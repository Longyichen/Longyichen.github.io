Overview

简介

Building more efficient and more scalable foundation model systems.

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Tingwen Liu at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research focuses on efficient architectures, training, and inference methods for foundation models, especially approaches grounded in the internal physical characteristics of large language models. Before my Ph.D., I received my bachelor's degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications through the Pei-Da-Ye Honors Program, and also obtained a first-class bachelor's degree from Queen Mary University of London.

I am currently a research intern in the Qwen Pretrain Team, advised by Bo Zheng, where I work on next-generation architecture research and efficiency optimization for ultra-large-scale models. Previously, I participated in multiple end-to-end foundation model training pipelines across both academia and industry: at ByteDance, I learned rigorous industrial research collaboration and project organization; at CAS, I built a solid NLP research methodology; at Baidu, I worked with large-scale compute and early trillion-scale systems such as ERNIE 4.5 and ERNIE 5.0; and at Ubiquant, I deepened my understanding of Megatron-style training and scaling-oriented optimization.

I am deeply interested in advanced architectures and theory for realizing AGI, including the physics of LLMs, model optimization and stability, reinforcement learning, and agent systems. I welcome conversations with anyone, whether online or over coffee in person. If you are a beginner, I would also be glad to share whatever experience and advice may be helpful.

构建更高效,更具可拓展性的大模型系统

我是中国科学院大学四年级博士生，导师是柳厅文教授，所在单位为中国科学院信息工程研究所。我的主要研究方向是面向基础模型的高效架构、训练与推理方法，尤其关注基于大语言模型内部物理特征的架构设计与效率优化。本科毕业于北京邮电大学，完成叶培大拔尖人才培养计划，并获得伦敦大学玛丽女王学院一等学士学位。

我目前在 Qwen Pretrain Team 担任研究实习生，指导老师为 Bo Zheng，主要进行新一代模型架构研究和超大规模模型的效率优化。此前，我在学术界与工业界多次参与基础模型全流程训练：在字节跳动，我系统理解了工业界科研协作与项目组织；在中科院，我建立了较完整的 NLP 研究方法；在百度，我开始接触大规模算力、系统化架构设计，以及ERNIE 4.5、ERNIE 5.0这类万亿级系统的早期研究；在Ubiquant，我进一步加深了对 Megatron 风格训练与 scaling-oriented optimization 的理解。

我对实现 AGI 的先进架构与理论非常感兴趣，其中包括 LLM 的物理学、模型优化与稳定性、强化学习、Agent 系统等等。我很欢迎任何人和我进行交流，不论线上还是线下约 coffee chat。如果您是初学者，我也很乐意给您提供力所能及的经验和建议。

Research研究

Research Questions and Directions研究问题与方向

A concise view of the questions that motivate my work and the directions I am actively pursuing.用更直接的方式概括我当前最关心的研究问题，以及正在推进的几个方向。

I strongly believe in scaling laws, and I aim to introduce generalizable inductive biases into model architectures so that both training and inference can become more efficient in a principled, cumulative way.

我一直都相信 scaling law，并且致力于在架构中植入一些通用且可泛化的 inductive bias，从而以更有原则的方式持续优化模型的训练与推理效率。

Core Questions核心问题

  1. Models remain fundamentally constrained by fitting and overfitting to data distributions.
  2. Token-level training and inference are still too inefficient.
  3. Scaling experiments still lack scientific yet actionable theoretical guidance.
  1. 模型始终受限于对数据分布的拟合与过拟合。
  2. 当前模型在 token-level 训练与推理上的效率仍然偏低。
  3. 模型缩放实验依然缺少科学且可实践的理论解释与指导。

Research Lens研究视角

From the perspective of large-scale pretraining, I care most about how architectural choices can unlock new scaling dimensions, reduce wasted computation, and make system-level optimization transferable across model families.

从大规模预训练的视角出发，我更关注架构选择如何打开新的缩放维度，如何减少无效计算，以及如何让系统级优化能够在不同模型家族之间迁移和复用。

Computational Ceiling计算上限

I study how to raise the computational ceiling of model architectures by improving their expressive power, including MoUE, ITT, PUT, and Sparse Growing Transformer.

我关注如何提升模型的计算表达能力上限，从而打开架构的能力天花板，代表性工作包括 MoUE、ITT、PUT 和 Sparse Growing Transformer。

Sparse Scaling稀疏缩放

I also study how multi-dimensional sparsity can open new scaling axes while preserving efficiency, as in MoUE, MoHD, and X-gram.

我也关注如何通过多维稀疏计算，在保持高效的同时打开新的模型拓展维度，例如 MoUE、MoHD 和 X-gram。

Parameter Insight参数洞察

A third direction uses insights into internal parameters to enable low-cost transformation and optimization, including DHA, APEX, LEMON, and NACL.

第三个方向是基于模型内部参数的洞察，以很低成本进行转化与优化，代表工作包括 DHA、APEX、LEMON 和 NACL。
